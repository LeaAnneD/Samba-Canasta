# Samba Canasta - Official Rules

## Game Overview

**Objective:** Create specific combinations of cards (melds) to score points. The team with the highest score wins.

**Players:** 4 players in 2 partnerships
**Decks:** 6 standard decks (324 cards total)
**Target Score:** Highest total (typical: 8,000-22,000 points)

## Card Values

| Card | Points |
|------|--------|
| Joker (Wild) | 50 |
| Ace | 20 |
| Two (Wild) | 20 |
| King-8 | 10 |
| 7-4 | 5 |
| Red Three | +100 (auto-collect) |
| Black Three | -100 (penalty in hand) |

## Setup

1. **Shuffle** all 6 decks together
2. **Determine first player** (highest card drawn)
3. **Deal:** Each player deals themselves 2 piles of 15 cards
4. **Exchange:** Pass piles to left and right opponents
5. **Choose:** Pick either left or right pile as Hand 1
6. **Hand 2:** Other pile stays face-down until after going down

## The Two-Hand System

- **Hand 1:** Start with 15 cards (visible)
- **Hand 2:** 15 cards face-down, can only pick up after going down
- **Going Down:** Meet minimum point requirement to meld
- **Set-Aside Cards:** When picking up discard pile, 7 cards set aside until next turn

## Going Down Requirements

| Round | Points Required |
|-------|----------------|
| 1 | 50 |
| 2 | 90 |
| 3 | 120 |
| 4 | 150* |

*Round 4 can be negotiated down by mutual agreement

## Turn Sequence

1. **Draw** 3 cards from stock OR pick up discard pile (8+ cards)
2. **Declare Red Threes** (if drawn) and replace
3. **Pick up Hand 2** (if eligible)
4. **Meld cards** (optional)
5. **Discard** 1 card

## Melds

### Groups (Canastas)
- 3+ cards of same rank
- **Complete (7 cards):**
  - Natural (no wilds): 500 pts
  - Seven 7s: 2,000 pts
  - Dirty (6 natural + 1 wild): 300 pts

### Sequences (Sambas)
- 3+ consecutive cards, same suit
- NO wild cards allowed
- **Complete (7 cards):** 2,000 pts
- Maximum 4 sequences (one per suit)

### Wild Card Group
- 7 wild cards (Jokers/2s): 2,000 pts

## Discard Pile Rules

**To Pick Up:**
- Pile must have 8+ cards
- Need 2 matching natural cards in hand
- Top card must be melded immediately with your 2 cards
- Remaining 7 cards set aside until next turn
- Cannot use Hand 2 cards for initial meld
- Must have gone down OR can go down with this pickup

**Black Three:** Blocks pile when discarded (next player cannot pick up)

## Going Out Requirements

**Must complete ALL:**
1. One 7-card sequence (Samba)
2. One group of seven 7s
3. One group of 7 wild cards
4. Five groups of 7 (same rank)
5. One dirty (6 natural + 1 wild)
6. Play all cards from hand

**Bonus:** +200 points if you go out before stock depletes

## Scoring

```
Team Score =
  Going Out Bonus (200 if applicable)
  + Completed Meld Bonuses
  + Card Values on Table
  + Red Threes (100 each)
  - Cards in Hand Penalty
```

### Meld Bonuses

| Meld | Bonus |
|------|-------|
| 7-card Sequence | 2,000 |
| Seven 7s | 2,000 |
| 7 Wild Cards | 2,000 |
| Natural Group of 7 | 500 |
| Dirty | 300 |

## Special Cards

**Red Threes:**
- Auto-collected when drawn
- Worth +100 points each
- Draw replacement immediately

**Black Threes:**
- Cannot be melded
- Worth -100 in hand at end
- Block discard pile when discarded

**Wild Cards (Jokers & 2s):**
- Can substitute in groups only
- Cannot be used in sequences
- Groups with wilds = Dirty (worth 300)

## Strategy Tips

1. **Save 7s** for groups (2,000 pts) not sequences
2. **Build required melds** early (sequences, 7s, wilds, dirty)
3. **Block opponents** with Black Threes when pile is 8+ cards
4. **Plan Hand 2** timing carefully
5. **Track requirements** to ensure you can go out
6. **Minimize hand** as game progresses

## Game End

Game ends when:
- A team goes out (meets all requirements)
- Stock pile depletes

**Winner:** Highest total score

## Quick Reference

**Turn:** Draw 3 → Meld → Discard 1
**Wild Cards:** Jokers & 2s
**Going Down:** 50/90/120/150 pts by round
**Going Out:** All 6 required melds + no cards in hand
**Bonus:** +200 for going out before stock depletes
